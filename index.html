How to play
Pass

Tap a numbered teammate (or press 1, 2 or 3), then stop the power in the green. Pick a lane the defenders aren't patrolling.

Shoot

Stop the crosshair to aim, then stop it again to set the power. Go above the bar and it flies over. Corners are hardest to save.

Tackle

Slide in when the ball is loose from his feet. Too early and he skips past. Mistime it and it's a foul or a miss.

Save

A red target flashes where he's aiming. Dive left, stay or dive right before the ball arrives. The arrow keys work too.

Free kicks

Get fouled in their half and you can go for goal. Aim around the wall, or set the height to curl it over and under the bar.

Corners

Attacking: stop the cross on the runner with the green ring, who has lost his marker. Defending: head it when the ball reaches the ring.

Squads

Every club has an XI with made-up names. Tap Squads to rename anyone, yourself and your mates included. Goals are credited to scorers, and seasons and cups keep a top scorers table.

Trophy cabinet

Leagues and cups you win go on the shelf, next to your records: biggest win, longest unbeaten run, best finish and more.

Draft

Build an XI from players across the Premier League, one position at a time, picking one of three each time. Name your team, choose a kit, then take on any club. Ratings move each week with the players' form.

Tactics

Pick them at kick-off and change them at half time. Attacking makes passing and shooting easier but tackles and saves harder. Defensive does the opposite.

Penalties

Get brought down in the box and it's a penalty. Commit a foul near your own box and they might get one.
